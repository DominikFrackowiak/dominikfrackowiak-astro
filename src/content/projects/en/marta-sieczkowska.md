# MARTA SIECZKOWSKA - PORTFOLIO

Marta Sieczkowska is an exceptionally talented illustrator and graphic designer. I had the pleasure of turning her precise creative direction into a minimalist portfolio website. The design called for full creative control, while Marta also needed a CMS for easy content management. I built the site with Next.js and connected it to a Headless WordPress API, giving the project a familiar backend without forcing the frontend into template limitations.
